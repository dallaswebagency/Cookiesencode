CookiesEncode is an AI, technology and digital growth company. We help businesses, startups and digital agencies solve real problems with the right mix of artificial intelligence, software engineering and marketing.

We started as Dallas Web Agency, building websites and white-label development for agencies around the world. As our clients' needs grew, so did we. Today we design AI agents and automated workflows, build SaaS platforms and custom software, and run search and paid growth programmes that keep pace with how people now find businesses, including through AI search.

Our clients are small and medium-sized businesses getting serious about automation, founders turning a product idea into an MVP, and agencies that need a dependable team behind their brand.

What ties it together is one team that understands strategy, engineering and growth. You don't need three vendors who don't talk to each other. You get practical advice, clear communication and work that's built to last.
